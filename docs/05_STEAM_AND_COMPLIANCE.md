# 05 — Steam Release and Compliance

Status key: **[D]** decided, **[P]** proposed, **[O]** open. Verify current Steam rules before submission; they change.

## 1. Release plan **[P]**
- Platform: Steam, Windows + Steam Deck **[D]**.
- Price: small, around EUR 5-10 **[P]**. Early Access is recommended **[P]**.
- Free demo for a Steam Next Fest **[P]**.
- Optional later: cosmetic DLC (outfit packs) **[O]**.

## 2. Content limits (internal rules, enforced on all art and text) **[D]**
Allowed: stylized "suggestive" fanservice such as lingerie, swimwear, cute/teasing poses.
Not allowed:
- Nudity (including implied or transparent clothing that reveals nudity).
- Explicit sexual content, sexual acts, or sexually explicit poses.
- Any character that looks like a minor. All characters are clearly adults (backstory age + design).
- Sexualized violence.
Reviewer checklist for each new outfit/art: if in doubt, make it more covered. The owner has the final say.
Reason: stay clearly out of adult-only territory to keep store access and payment processor compatibility safe. Do not tag the game as sexual content or nudity in the store survey if the content is not.

## 3. AI content disclosure (Steam) **[D to comply]**
- Steam asks developers to disclose generative AI used for content that ships to players, including art, sound, narrative, and store/marketing assets; the disclosure is shown on the store page.
- Two categories exist: content generated during development (pre-generated) and content generated while the game runs (live-generated). **This project uses only pre-generated AI content. No live-generated AI in the game.** **[D]**
- Development-only uses that do not ship (code assistants, ideation concept art that is never shipped) are treated differently by Steam's current guidance; confirm the current wording on the Steam developer docs before submission.
- Obligation: the content must not be illegal or infringing, and the store page promises must match the game.
- The asset manifest (`03_ART_PIPELINE.md`) is the source of truth for the disclosure text.

## 4. Intellectual property risks **[P]**
- Assets generated by AI with little human creative input may have weak or no copyright protection in some jurisdictions. Mitigation: human curation, hand touch-ups on key characters, keep the game's name, characters and design documents as human-authored IP, and keep provenance records.
- Do not prompt with real artists' names or existing copyrighted characters. Do not use models/LoRAs trained to copy a specific living artist or franchise.
- Check model licenses for commercial use before each model enters the pipeline; record in the manifest.
